Our story

SLAY_HAIRBEAUTY was founded in 2024 by Akhokhele Madikwa. During her Gap Year as a sourse of income for her. She had no idea that SLAY_HAIRBEAUTY would grow into what it is today , with a good rate of clients. There has been a huge amount of women who find their comfort and confidence through wigs and thats what slay_hairbeauty is here for. To provide high quality wigs at a afforable price and wig maintenance.
